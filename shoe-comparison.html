How to read this. The 1 to 5 meters are our own scales built from published specifications. Downturn: flat is 1, moderate is 3, aggressive is 5. Stiffness: extremely low is 1, low is 2, medium-low is 3, medium is 4, high is 5. These describe how a shoe is built, not how well it performs.

Sources and limits. Specifications and MSRPs (US dollars) come from Treeline Review’s May 2026 bouldering shoe comparison table, retrieved 8 November 2026. The Butora Gomi’s upper material is from REI’s product listing. The Scarpa Origin’s specifications and price are from Scarpa’s US product page. The Scarpa Force V’s come from retailer listings of Scarpa’s specs, and its price is a past MSRP, because Scarpa is replacing it with the Scarpa Force. *Stiffness for those two is our estimate from midsole specs, since no source rates it. Prices change, so check the retailer. The level groupings are CRUX’s own, based on downturn, stiffness and price. We haven’t tested these shoes ourselves yet, so there are no performance scores here. Try shoes on before you buy, because fit differs between brands and feet.
