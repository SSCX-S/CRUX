Beginner guide

Start here.

Your first weeks of climbing, in the order that helps most. No jargon, no pressure.

New to the sport? Read the Introduction to Climbing

Before your first session

  • Take your gym’s induction or intro session. Staff will show you the rules and how to fall safely.
  • Wear clothes you can move and stretch in, and bring water.
  • Rent shoes at first. You don’t need to buy anything yet.
  • Never walk or stand under someone who is climbing, and always follow the gym’s rules.
  • Warm up for 10 to 15 minutes before you start pulling hard.

Your first four skills

  1. 01

    Silent feet

    Look at the hold until your toe lands, then place it without scraping. Standing on your feet saves your arms.

  2. 02

    Reading problems

    Find the start and finish holds, then plan your hand and foot sequence before you leave the floor.

  3. 03

    Hips and balance

    Turn side-on to the wall and keep your weight over your feet so your arms can relax.

  4. 04

    Resting and recovery

    Shake out between attempts, breathe, and take rest days. Fingers and tendons adapt more slowly than muscles.

Detailed guides for each skill are coming soon.

Your first four weeks

  • Week 1Learn the gym rules, warm up properly and climb easy problems just to get used to moving on the wall.
  • Week 2Focus on footwork: place every foot deliberately and try not to scrape or readjust.
  • Week 3Add route reading. Look at each problem from the floor before you climb it.
  • Week 4Take it easier, repeat your favourites and notice what has improved.

Gear for beginners

  • Shoes: rent until you are climbing regularly, then buy a comfortable, flat or slightly downturned pair. Snug is fine, but they shouldn’t hurt.
  • Chalk and a brush: optional at first, but worth having once your hands get sweaty.
  • Everything else: you don’t need it yet. Skip finger boards and extra kit until you have climbed consistently for a while.
