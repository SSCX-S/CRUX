Four types, side by side

Loose chalk

Fine powder in a bag or tub.

Airborne dust
Very high
Mess in your bag
Very high
Speed to apply
Very fast
Gym rules
Often restricted

Best for: Climbers who chalk heavily, mostly outdoors.

Examples: Metolius Super Chalk, Black Diamond White Gold, Trango Gunpowder

Chunky chalk

Crushed pieces you can break down to the texture you like.

Airborne dust
High
Mess in your bag
High
Speed to apply
Fast
Gym rules
Often restricted

Best for: Climbers who want control over the texture.

Examples: Petzl Power Crunch, Friction Labs Gorilla Grip

Chalk ball

A refillable sock that releases chalk as you squeeze.

Airborne dust
Low
Mess in your bag
Low
Speed to apply
Fast
Gym rules
Often allowed

Best for: Gym climbers and anyone who wants less mess.

Examples: Metolius Refillable Super Chalk Sock

Liquid chalk

A liquid that dries to a chalk layer on your skin.

Airborne dust
Very low
Mess in your bag
Low
Speed to apply
Slow
Gym rules
Usually allowed

Best for: A base layer before climbing, and gyms that restrict loose chalk.

Examples: Mammut Liquid Chalk, Friction Labs Secret Stuff

How to read this. The meters are our own 1 to 5 scales describing how each type generally works. They are not measurements, and individual products vary. Gym rules differ, so always check yours before you buy.

Sources and limits. Additive and resealable details for the Metolius Super Chalk, Black Diamond White Gold, Trango Gunpowder, Petzl Power Crunch, Mammut Liquid Chalk and Frank Endo block come from OutdoorGearLab’s chalk specification tables. Product names, forms and prices for the rest come from REI’s climbing chalk listing. Both were retrieved 8 November 2026. REI prices cover the sizes it sells, so they are ranges and not per-ounce prices, and where an item was on sale we show the regular price. “Not stated” means we couldn’t confirm it from those sources. The meters show each product’s type profile from the section above, not a measurement of the product itself. We haven’t tested these chalks, so there are no performance scores.
