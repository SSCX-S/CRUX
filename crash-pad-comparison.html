How to read this. Landing area is the open length times the open width, worked out from the listed dimensions. Bars compare the pads on this page, not every pad on the market. A bigger or thicker pad isn’t automatically safer, because where and how you fall matters more, so read the safety notes in the guide.

Sources and limits. Dimensions, thickness, weight and fold type come from the manufacturer specifications in Treeline Review’s April 2026 bouldering crash pad comparison, retrieved 8 November 2026. Foam, hip belt and carrying details are from the same review’s descriptions. Prices are MSRPs in US dollars. We left the price blank for the Organic Simple Pad and the Black Diamond Circuit because the source lists two different prices for each, and for the Moon Warrior’s weight for the same reason. We haven’t tested these pads ourselves, so there are no protection or comfort scores. A pad never replaces spotting, good landing technique or sensible choices about height.
